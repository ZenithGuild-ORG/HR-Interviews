# We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer

ZenithGuild is a remote team building software for clients worldwide. We're hiring a senior developer to lead client interviews across all our team's projects — the person who sits with a client, works out what they actually need, and translates it into something our engineers can build.

## What you'd do

- Run discovery and requirements interviews with clients on behalf of the whole team
- Turn those conversations into clear technical specs, scope, and acceptance criteria
- Act as the technical point of contact for client questions during delivery
- Diagnose reported issues, reproduce them, and hand engineers a well-scoped ticket
- Write up decisions, integration notes, and internal guides so the work is repeatable

## What we're looking for

- 5+ years building and shipping software, with real ownership of technical decisions
- Solid grounding in cloud infrastructure, APIs, and system integration
- Proven experience in client-facing or consulting work — you're comfortable leading a call, pushing back on a bad idea
- Ability to move between a client conversation and a codebase in the same afternoon
- Excellent spoken and written English
- Self-directed and organised across multiple client engagements at once

## Nice to have

- Hands-on experience with LLMs, RAG, or agent frameworks (we'll fill gaps, but you should know the landscape)
- Background in pre-sales, solutions architecture, or technical account management

## Terms

Flexible hours, fully remote, working directly with the people building the product.
